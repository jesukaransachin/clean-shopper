Logo Variants

Six directions, all built from the design system palette (brand green → amber gradient) and Inter Extrabold wordmark. Pick one to move forward with, or mix an icon with a different wordmark treatment.

A · Leaf + Checkmark
Current direction. Leaf silhouette + checkmark stroke — "natural" and "verified" in one mark.
B · Verified Shield
Leans harder into trust/safety — a shield reads as protection from harmful ingredients.
C · Bag + Leaf
Literal nod to "Shopper" — a bag with a leaf inside signals both commerce and clean ingredients.
D · Monogram "C" + Sprout
Simplified, scales down best for a small favicon — a "C" with a small leaf sprouting off it.
E · Wordmark Only
No icon — gradient-filled text. Simplest option, works well in tight header spaces.
F · Droplet + Checkmark
"Clean" read literally as purity/water — a droplet shape with the same verified checkmark.
